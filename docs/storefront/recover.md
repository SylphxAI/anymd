# Recover your anymd Pro licence

Use the email address you paid with to request a recovery link. For your privacy,
this page won't reveal whether an email has an order. Open the link in your email
to view your licence.

Open the recovery link within one hour to view and copy your licence token,
then activate it on your machine:

```sh
anymd pro activate '<your token>'
anymd pro status
```

For a server or CI, use `ANYMD_PRO_TOKEN`. Recovering your licence does not change
its plan, allowance or expiry, or who can use it. Do not share a licence token or
recovery link in a public issue, log or repository.
