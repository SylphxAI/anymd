## Check a PDF quote where it was cited

Check up to **100 citations per request** against their cited page and area. See whether each quote matches, doesn't match, or needs more evidence. Checks use the text and its position on the page, not an AI opinion.

A match confirms where a quote appears, not whether it is true. Scans without OCR or missing text positions may leave a citation unconfirmed, and OCR errors can still affect a result. [See the API inputs and verdicts](/docs/cite-check).

## See the video moment behind a claim

Find your way through scenes, chapters and subtitle or transcript timings. Each timeline is linked to its source file. View a frame with both the requested time and the actual decoded timestamp, so you can check which moment it shows.

Timelines cover **up to 10 minutes per request**. Scene markers detect visual cuts; they do not explain what the video means. No AI model download is needed. [Explore video timelines and frame extraction](/docs/video-timeline).

## FAQ

**What does a citation match tell me?**
It tells you that the quoted text was found on the specified page and area. It does not establish that the statement is true. Missing OCR or text positions can leave a citation unconfirmed.
